## Second adult organizer

Required by the safety design. Record privately, not in this repository.

- Name, phone, arrival time, responsibilities, backup plan.
- **If no second adult is confirmed by September 4, the event does not proceed
  as designed.** Recruit one or postpone.

## Event kit

- [ ] Phone with charger or power bank
- [ ] Attendee-interest list, printed or on phone
- [ ] Small λ / KNP table sign

# First event runbook

Lambda House Kanpur #01 — Sunday, September 6, 2026, 4:00–6:00 PM.

Target 20–25 registrations, plan for 8–15 attendees. If registrations exceed
safe venue capacity, move to a larger venue rather than crowding the room.

## Run of show

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 3:30 PM | Organizers arrive, check the space, arrange seating, place the table sign |
| 4:00 PM | Informal arrivals. Greet each person individually |
| 4:10 PM | Two-minute welcome and norms |
| 4:15 PM | Names and one current tech curiosity, one sentence each |
| 4:30 PM | Open conversation |
| 5:15 PM | Optional movement between tables |
| 5:50 PM | Build-session window announced, brief feedback asked |
| 6:00 PM | Official close. People may stay independently |

## Welcome script

    Thanks for coming to the first Lambda House meetup.

    I started this because I wanted a place in Kanpur where people could talk
    about technology naturally and meet others who are just as curious.

    You do not need a particular job, degree, age, or level of experience to
    belong here. Today is deliberately simple. We'll each share our name and
    one technology we are curious about, then let the conversations develop.

    Keep it tech-related, respect people, ask before privately messaging or
    photographing anyone, and help newcomers enter the conversation.

## Attendance

Record quietly, without turning arrival into a checkpoint: display name,
attended, tech interest, photo preference, first-event status. Do not demand new
personal information from walk-ins.

Use `templates/event-attendance.csv`. Keep it private.

## Organizer conduct on the day

- Two adult organizers present from setup to close.
- Introduce people to each other based on the interests they submitted. This is
  the single highest-value thing the organizer does.
- Watch for anyone standing alone and bring them into a conversation.
- Step back once conversation is running. Do not dominate.
- Photograph only with consent, and never publish an identifiable photo of an
  under-18 attendee.

## Feedback questions

Ask in conversation, or with a minimal form. Feedback is never a condition of
belonging.

1. Did you meet someone you would like to speak with again?
2. What made the meetup comfortable or uncomfortable?
3. What should the first build session make room for?

## Recap post

Include: exact turnout, two or three conversation themes, the next-event window,
and consented photographs only.

Never: identify minors, quote private conversations, inflate attendance, or
imply sponsorship.

## If things go wrong

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Very few register | Run it anyway for whoever commits. A good first meetup with six people beats a cancelled one |
| Many register, few attend | Judge success by the people present. Use personal confirmation 48 hours ahead next time |
| Venue cancels | Activate the backup, message every RSVP immediately with the new address |
| Nobody talks | Ask a question to the room from the submitted interests. Split into smaller groups sooner |
| Someone dominates | Redirect with a direct question to someone quieter |
| Conversation turns career-only | Redirect toward tools, ideas, and projects. Do not ban legitimate career talk |
| A safety incident | Follow `06-conduct-safety-moderation.md`. Safety first, documentation after |

# Conduct, safety, and moderation

The organizer playbook. The public version of the rules lives at `/conduct`;
this file adds the procedure behind them.

## The ten public rules

1. Curiosity is enough. Do not gatekeep by age, credentials, experience,
   employer, college, or technical ability.
2. Keep conversation related to technology.
3. Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
4. No harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content, doxxing, or invasive
   behaviour.
5. Ask before privately messaging another member.
6. Share your work to start a conversation, not to repeatedly advertise.
7. No unsolicited recruiting, course promotion, fundraising, investment
   pitches, or mass forwards.
8. Respect photography preferences and safeguards for younger members.
9. Do not publish another member's messages, identity, or contact information
   without permission.
10. Organizers may remove content or members to protect the community.

## Enforcement ladder

1. **Minor first issue** — private reminder quoting the specific rule.
2. **Repeated or more serious** — written warning stating the observable
   behaviour and the change expected.
3. **Continued** — removal from group and events.
4. **Harassment, threats, predatory behaviour, doxxing, or a serious safety
   breach** — immediate removal, and reporting where the law requires it.
5. Serious decisions use two organizers, once a second organizer exists.
6. Never debate identifiable incident details in public.

## Event safeguards

- Public, staffed venue, reasonable hours.
- Two adult organizers present from setup through official close.
- No closed-room one-to-one sessions.
- No home meetups, no overnight events.
- No community-arranged private transport involving minors.
- No adult-initiated private contact with minors.
- No identifiable under-18 photography by default.
- Walk-ins may attend, subject to venue capacity.
- Private reporting route visible on the conduct page and in every event
  reminder.

## Response scripts

**Minor reminder**

    I'm messaging privately because this conflicts with the Lambda House rule:
    [QUOTE THE EXACT RULE]. Please stop this behaviour so the group can remain
    comfortable for everyone.

**Warning**

    This is a formal warning under the Lambda House conduct policy. The
    behaviour was: [STATE THE OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR WITHOUT SPECULATION].
    Repeating it will result in removal.

**Removal**

    Your access to Lambda House is being removed because of: [STATE THE RULE
    AND DOCUMENTED BEHAVIOUR]. Do not contact members through information
    obtained from the community.

## Scenario responses

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated course promotion | Rule 7. Private reminder, then warning, then removal. Delete the messages. |
| Unsolicited DM after being asked to stop | Rule 5. Straight to written warning; this is already the second issue by definition. |
| Adult-initiated private contact with a minor | Immediate removal. Preserve evidence. Assess reporting obligation under POCSO. Do not negotiate. |
| Harassment during an event | Stop it in the room first, involve venue staff if needed, separate the people, then immediate removal. Support the person harassed before documenting. |
| Photograph posted against a recorded preference | Remove it within the hour, tell the person it is down, then a reminder or warning depending on intent. |
| Credible immediate threat | Safety first: contact emergency services, involve venue staff, remove access. Documentation comes after people are safe. |
| Disagreement about a technical opinion | Not a conduct issue. Do not moderate. Intervene only if rule 3 is broken. |

Record observable facts, not speculation about motive. Real incidents are stored
**outside this repository**, with access limited to Akshat and one designated
organizer.

## Legal review trigger

An Indian legal professional must review this policy before Lambda House runs
youth-specific programs, private mentoring, institutional partnerships
involving children, or anything beyond public social gatherings. Until that
review returns in writing, do not describe the policy as legally reviewed.
